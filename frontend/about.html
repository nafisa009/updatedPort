About Me

Software Engineering student, builder, and lifelong curious mind.

I'm Nafisa Nasrin, a Software Engineering student at Daffodil International University, currently in my journey since 2024. Like an archaeologist piecing together ancient history, I enjoy digging into problems, breaking them apart, and building something meaningful out of the pieces — whether that's a web app, a game, or a tool that makes someone's day a little easier.
